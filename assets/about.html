About me

I am a software engineer!

I haven't spent much time working in any one technology in recent years. In fact, I'm used to switching between different technologies for the needs of my projects. Because of this, it's sometimes difficult for me to master a particular language in depth, but I've learned to adapt quickly to be efficient.

From April 2021 to August 2024, I worked on different phases of the Mosaique project, which allows SNCF agents to sell tickets at station ticket offices. My ability to adapt to different technologies (VB6, C#, Powershell, etc.) allowed me to quickly join a development team while handling daily integration tasks. Later, I served as the liaison between the various departments involved at various levels of the project. When my company was experiencing difficulties, I researched the emerging industries in my region and chose Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality because it particularly appealed to me.

The program offered by Holberton School allowed me to realize my desire to reorient myself in this field.

When I volunteered to carry out the "Volunteer Management" project for the Léa association, I quickly took charge of the project. I realized that I finally had enough confidence and maturity to lead a project. I set the pace for the team, schedule meetings with our client and try to motivate my teammates to stay on track.
